Arqelin

Arqelin is an AI-powered customer resolution and follow-through platform designed to help businesses manage customer issues from report to verified resolution.

What Arqelin Does

- Helps organize and categorize customer issues
- Supports intelligent case routing and escalation
- Tracks pending actions and customer promises
- Sends reminders for important follow-ups
- Helps keep customers appropriately updated
- Detects recurring problems and potential delays
- Assists support teams with AI-powered suggestions
- Supports cases through to verified resolution

Our Mission

To help businesses ensure that customer problems, important actions, and promises do not get lost between "We'll handle it" and "It's actually resolved."

Status

🚧 Arqelin is currently in the early development stage.
